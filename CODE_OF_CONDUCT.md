# Community code of conduct

Rails CVE should be a welcoming place to improve security tooling together.

Be respectful, assume good intent where reasonable, and keep feedback specific to the work. Make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Clear disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, and sharing someone else's private information without consent are not acceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

Report concerns privately to [dev@avi.nyc](mailto:dev@avi.nyc). Maintainers will review reports and handle personal information with care. Depending on the circumstances, they may edit or remove content, ask someone to change their behavior, or restrict participation. Maintainers are held to the same expectations.
